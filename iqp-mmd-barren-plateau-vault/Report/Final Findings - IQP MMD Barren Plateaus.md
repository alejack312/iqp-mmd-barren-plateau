# Final Findings - IQP MMD Barren Plateaus

> [!abstract]
> This is the closing thesis-chapter draft for the project. It consolidates the Hypothesis, Forge, Qiskit, anti-concentration, marginal, Grid5000, and Pauli-estimator work into one evidence-backed narrative. The raw artifact index is in [[Evidence Ledger]], and the extra structural mining pass is in [[Pattern Mining Results]].

## 1. Research Tension

The project started from a three-way tension:

- IQP circuits are the sampling-hard model class that motivates quantum deployment.
- IQP-MMD can be trained classically through closed-form or sampled expectation estimators.
- Variational quantum models often exhibit barren plateaus, so trainability cannot be assumed.

The final evidence says the tension does not resolve into one yes/no statement. The answer depends on which property is being measured:

| Axis | What We Found |
|---|---|
| Gradient/plateau labels | Init and system size dominate. Structure matters mainly through a complete-graph escape hatch. |
| Forge predicate | The frozen F4 rule classifies `277 / 283` training plus holdout rows correctly, with no false positives. |
| Anti-concentration | Strict AC alone is too weak as a learned-distribution success criterion. |
| Marginals | Target-vs-learned marginal agreement is the better diagnostic for whether IQP-MMD learned the relevant structure. |
| Qiskit | Statevector expectations match the classical IQP path at machine precision on the report smoke run; finite shots and noise introduce visible estimator error. |
| Scale | Exact distribution diagnostics are feasible at small n; native large-n work needs estimator diagnostics and real compute. |

## 2. Hypothesis Layer

The Hypothesis work was not a standalone experiment. It hardened the experiment surface before the larger runs:

- SMART family generators satisfy their intended invariants: product-state identity, square-lattice nearest-neighbor edges, Erdos-Renyi unique two-body edges, and complete-graph all-pairs coverage.
- Full MMD instances generated by `mmd_instance()` keep loss and analytic gradients finite across sampled family/kernel/init/data combinations.
- The init layer is explicitly tested: uniform, small-angle, and data-dependent inits all materialize finite theta vectors with the correct generator shape.

Interpretation: Hypothesis did not discover the final scientific rule, but it reduced the chance that Forge or AC patterns were artifacts of malformed `G`, bad theta shape, or non-finite MMD evaluations.

## 3. Forge and Plateau Patterns

Forge produced the cleanest rule-like finding.

The failed baseline was F3:

> A purely structural predicate, `bounded_degree[3] AND high_overlap[2]`, had `0 / 88` recall on observed plateaus.

That failure was informative. The missing axis was init. F4 added init and n:

```text
plateau if small_angle
or plateau if uniform and n >= 6 and not complete_graph_like
```

The final combined score across the training sweep and two holdouts:

| Rule | Rows | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | FP | FN |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| F4 current | 283 | 97.9% | 100.0% | 97.5% | 0 | 6 |
| Init+n only, no escape | 283 | 90.8% | 92.1% | 97.5% | 20 | 6 |
| Lower threshold to n >= 4, no escape | 283 | 84.1% | 84.1% | 100.0% | 45 | 0 |
| Lower threshold to n >= 4, keep complete-graph escape | 283 | 92.9% | 92.2% | 100.0% | 20 | 0 |

The structural escape hatch is load-bearing. Removing `not complete_graph_like` creates exactly the complete-graph uniform false positives seen in training and extended-n holdout.

The remaining miss is narrow: `uniform x n=4`. The six misses are two training `erdos_renyi` rows and four unseen-family `symmetric` rows. The new mining pass found no simple one-feature structural threshold that closes this corner cleanly; the best `uniform x n=4` threshold reaches full recall but only `42.9%` precision. F4.1 should therefore be treated as an open enrichment problem, not a trivial threshold change.

## 4. Anti-Concentration and Marginals

The anti-concentration track changed the project framing.

Strict AC asks whether one distribution is spread enough. The supervisor's scientific question is closer to whether the learned distribution matches the target's concentration and marginals. Those are different.

Headline findings:

- AC12 bandwidth sweep: every learned distribution in the exact `n=9` binary-mixture sweep is much smoother than the target, even when MMD becomes small.
- AC11 Ghosh-Kim exact cell: learned scaled second moment stays below target in all exact `n=9` bandwidth cells.
- AC11 large sampled cell: concentration can move either direction depending on bandwidth, target, and sampling mode.
- Grid5000 native paper-fidelity runs: learned IQP-MMD distributions are concentrated, not Porter-Thomas-like anti-concentrated, because the paper targets themselves are concentrated.
- Ising is the cleanest learned target; blobs is harder; native genomic-805 shows good low-order marginal estimates through order 8.

Key exact/sampled AC rows:

| Run | n | Dataset | Sigma | Target S | Learned S | Target - Learned |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AC11 exact | 9 | binary_mixture | 1 | 70.984 | 20.328 | 50.657 |
| AC11 exact | 9 | binary_mixture | 3 | 100.660 | 4.828 | 95.833 |
| AC11 exact | 9 | binary_mixture | 9 | 85.141 | 12.125 | 73.015 |
| AC11 sampled | 20 | ising | 1 | 1030.000 | 1287.812 | -257.812 |
| AC11 sampled | 20 | ising | 3 | 1030.000 | 408.875 | 621.125 |
| AC11 sampled | 20 | ising | 9 | 1024.000 | 12039.344 | -11015.344 |

Interpretation: reporting "AC passes" can be true and still scientifically unhelpful. The report should cite scaled-second-moment magnitude, target-vs-learned gap, and per-order marginal error `M_k`.

## 6. Final Claims

Supported claims:

1. Structure-only plateau prediction is insufficient on this dataset.
2. The joint F4 rule is the strongest empirical plateau predicate found: init dominates, n matters, and complete-graph structure supplies a real escape hatch.
3. The only stable F4 miss pattern is `uniform x n=4`; simple structural thresholds do not close it cleanly.
4. Learned-distribution AC must not be reported without target comparison and marginal diagnostics.
5. IQP-MMD can learn concentrated target structure, but that does not preserve the naive anti-concentration story needed for sampling-hardness motivation.
6. Statevector Qiskit agrees with the classical IQP expectation implementation on the report smoke cross-check; finite shots/noise remain a separate practical limitation.

Unsupported or limited claims:

- F4 is not a theorem and is not validated outside the 283-row empirical base.
- Qiskit smoke results do not establish large-n hardware behavior.
- Pauli estimator validation is useful but has documented failure modes under extreme concentration.
- Native genomic results are estimator-based; exact enumeration is impossible at `n=805`.
